CANChat Agent Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Summary: CANChat Agent processes browser content only as needed to perform actions requested by the user. Relevant page content may be sent to the AI service configured for the extension. CANChat Agent does not sell personal information or use browser content for advertising.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how the CANChat Agent browser extension ("CANChat Agent", "the extension", "we", or "our") handles information when you install and use the extension in a Chromium-based browser such as Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.

2. Information the extension may process

Depending on the action you request and the permissions granted to the extension, CANChat Agent may process:

  • Text and other content from the web page you are actively using.
  • Page titles, URLs, links, and document structure needed to understand or navigate a page.
  • Content that you explicitly select, enter, upload, or ask the extension to analyze.
  • Browser state needed to perform requested actions, such as the active tab or navigation state.
  • Locally stored extension settings, preferences, and configuration information.
  • Information returned by tools, websites, or services that you direct the extension to use.

CANChat Agent is not designed to collect browsing history unrelated to a user-requested action.

3. How information is used

Information is processed only for purposes associated with providing the extension's functionality, including:

  • Answering questions about the current page or selected content.
  • Summarizing, extracting, transforming, or analyzing information.
  • Navigating websites or performing browser actions requested by the user.
  • Providing citations or references to source material.
  • Operating extension features such as local knowledge, tools, or browser automation.
  • Maintaining user settings and extension configuration.

4. AI services and data transmission

Some CANChat Agent features require sending information to an artificial intelligence service or model endpoint. When this occurs, the extension sends only the information reasonably required to complete the action requested by the user.

The specific AI provider or endpoint may depend on how the extension has been configured by the user or by the organization deploying it. Data sent to an external service is also subject to the privacy, security, retention, and contractual terms applicable to that service.

Users should not submit sensitive, classified, protected, confidential, or personal information to an AI service unless that service has been approved for the applicable information classification and use case.

5. Browser permissions

CANChat Agent may request browser permissions that allow it to access the active page, read page content, communicate with configured services, store settings, or perform browser actions.

These permissions are used to provide extension functionality and are not intended to enable unrelated tracking or advertising.

6. Authentication and credentials

CANChat Agent may detect whether a website appears to require authentication so that it can interact appropriately with the page. The extension is not intended to collect, record, or transmit passwords entered into websites.

Authentication tokens, API keys, or other credentials used to configure external services should be protected using the security controls provided by the extension and the browser. Users and administrators are responsible for ensuring that configured credentials are authorized for their intended use.

7. Local storage

The extension may store settings, preferences, cached information, local knowledge-base content, or other operational data in browser-managed storage on the user's device.

Locally stored data remains on the device unless a feature explicitly requires it to be sent to a configured service or the user takes an action that causes it to be transmitted.

8. Telemetry and diagnostics

CANChat Agent does not use browser content for advertising or cross-site behavioral profiling.

If diagnostic logging or telemetry is enabled in a particular deployment, information collected should be limited to what is necessary for security, reliability, troubleshooting, performance monitoring, or service management. Organizational deployments may apply additional logging and audit requirements.

9. Data sharing

CANChat Agent does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to service providers or systems only when required to perform a feature requested by the user, support the operation of the extension, meet organizational security or audit requirements, or comply with applicable law.

10. Data retention

Data stored locally by the extension remains subject to browser storage and extension configuration. Removing extension data or uninstalling the extension may remove locally stored information.

Information sent to an external AI service, logging service, or other system is retained according to the policies and configuration of that service or system.

11. Security

CANChat Agent is designed to limit access to browser information to what is needed for requested functionality. Users should review extension permissions, use approved AI endpoints, protect credentials, and follow applicable organizational security requirements.

12. Third-party websites and services

The extension may interact with websites, APIs, AI providers, or other services selected by the user or configured by an administrator. Those third parties operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

13. Children's privacy

CANChat Agent is not specifically directed to children and is intended for general or organizational productivity use.

14. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the extension's functionality, data handling practices, or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
